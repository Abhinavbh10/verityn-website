How it works

Four things that make
Verityn different.

01

"Why it matters to you"

Every story comes with a line written for your life in Germany. Not a generic summary. "Your Krankenkasse premium rises €18-25/month. Most plans adjust in January. Watch your December letter." Two sentences. Specific. Practical. That line is the whole product.

02

Seven stories. Three tiers.

The lead story gets the full treatment: headline, why-line, and a link to the source. Stories 2-4 are "Also Today." Stories 5-7 are "Worth Knowing." A headline and one sentence. You always know what to read first.

03

Tap "Deep Dive" on anything.

See a story that matters? Tap once and get the full picture: what happened, the background, why it matters, what different sides are saying, and what to watch next. Like asking a friend who actually follows German politics.

04

Your city, not just your country.

Pick Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, or Frankfurt. Verityn pulls local stories from your city. S-Bahn closures, housing rules, Anmeldung changes. Then blends them with nationwide news. 2-3 of your 7 stories come from your city. The rest cover what's happening across Germany.
